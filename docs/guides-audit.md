# Guides audit before launch

Review of the 55 published guides on northbankcleaning.co.uk (15 October 2026), to decide which to keep, merge or hold back before the site goes live.

## Summary

| Decision | Guides | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Keep | 42 | Published at launch. |
| Merge | 7 | Useful parts folded into stronger guides or service pages; old URLs 301 to targets. |
| Republished | 13 | Same WordPress posts, **new topics and slugs** (see below); published via `wp cc guides_republish`. |
| Delete outright | 0 | Every post was reused rather than deleted. |

**Why:** the guides are well structured (about 1,000–1,400 words each, clear headings, useful internal links), but there are several pairs that answer the same question. Pairs like that compete with each other in Google, so neither ranks well. A few also repeat what the service pages already say. A brand-new domain launching with 55 guides published on the same day also looks like bulk content; a smaller, stronger set plus a steady release schedule looks like a real business.

All 12 services on the site are offered at launch, so every guide supporting them is kept.

**Status:** applied on 15 October 2026 (see [What was done](#what-was-done)).

## Other findings

- **All 55 guides are dated 14 October 2026.** Don't backdate them. Publish the launch set on launch day and release the held-back guides one a week; that steady rhythm is healthier than everything appearing at once.
- **Service page titles don't mention the area.** For example, the After Builders Cleaning page's title is "After Builders Cleaning – Northbank Cleaning". Titles such as "After Builders Cleaning in Hull & East Yorkshire | Northbank Cleaning" would help local rankings far more than extra guides. *Done:* service pages now use this format, and area pages use "Cleaners in {town} | Northbank Cleaning".
- **Rank Math SEO.** Rank Math was installed on 15 October but was never set up, so it printed nothing. Because it was installed, the theme had also switched its own SEO tags off. It's now set up without a Rank Math account:
  - It uses the theme's SEO titles and descriptions unless you set one in its own panel.
  - Its schema module is off, because the theme's structured data covers every page type.
  - The sitemap is at `/sitemap_index.xml`. It lists guides, pages, services, areas and town pages, but not drafts, Bricks templates, authors or categories.
  - Main pages have titles and descriptions in Rank Math's per-page fields.
- **Links are in good shape.** No links point to the old temporary address, and the town-level service links all work. Each guide has 10–14 internal links, which is at the upper end; trim links to merged or held-back guides when making the changes.
- **Authorship is fine.** Guides show "By the Northbank Cleaning team" and your login name isn't exposed. Once real staff exist, a named author with a short bio would add credibility.
- **Encoding is fine.** Apostrophes in titles are stored correctly.

## What was done

Database backup taken first: `~/backups/guides-merge-<timestamp>/db.sql.gz` on the server.

**Merges into guides.** Each new section sits next to the related material in the target guide:
- How to Choose a Cleaner gained a "12 questions to ask a cleaning company before you book" section, just before "Red flags", plus 2 FAQs.
- How to Get Your Full Tenancy Deposit Back gained the wear-and-tear table, a paragraph on how adjudicators decide, a "Common mistakes that lose cleaning disputes" subsection and 2 FAQs. Its explanation of the deposit schemes already covered the rest.
- Deep Clean vs Regular Clean gained "How often should you deep clean?", the task-by-task schedule, "Signs your home needs a deep clean now" and 1 FAQ.
- How to Clean an Oven gained the usage schedule, "Signs your oven needs cleaning", "Simple habits" and 2 FAQs.

**Merges into service pages** (FAQs, added before the insurance and guarantee questions):
- Oven Cleaning: timings for single, double and range cookers, what happens during a clean, how to prepare.
- After Builders Cleaning: how long it takes, how it differs from a deep clean, why dust comes back.
- Regular House Cleaning: how often, how long each visit takes, whether to tidy first.

**Links.**
- 59 links on 40 pages pointed at the 13 guides. Each sentence was reworded by hand: links to merged guides now point to the guide or service page that replaced them, and sentences that only recommended a held-back guide were removed.
- "Related guides" lists were updated on 30 guides, and the four lists left nearly empty were topped up with close matches.

**Status and redirects.**
- The 7 merged guide **old URLs** still 301 to their replacement (Rank Math / `cc_merged_guides()`).
- The 13 former merge/hold-back posts were **repurposed** with new slugs (seed: `seed/guides/republished.php`, command: `wp cc guides_republish`). Old slugs are free for redirects; new URLs are live guides (55 published total).

**Republished topics (old slug → new slug):** see `seed/guides/republished-meta.php`.

**Warning:** don't re-run `wp cc guides` without `--only`. Full import skips republished slugs but still overwrites other guides from seed.
