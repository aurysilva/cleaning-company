| After builders cleaning Hull | Lightowler, Crystal Clear, C2 Humber, Mopli, GRPL | Local firms with dedicated service pages |

### What the ranking sites do well (we must match these)

- **One dedicated page per service per town** (e.g. `/end-of-tenancy-cleaning-hull`, `/deep-cleaning-beverley`).
- **Published prices.** Tenancy Cleaner even publishes real job data ("median Hull end of tenancy job £397.50", room-by-room price tables). Oven Gleaming publishes a fixed price list (single oven £50, double £65, AGA £115).
- **Guarantees:** "Deposit-back guarantee", "free 72-hour re-clean".
- **Very local copy:** Tenancy Cleaner references the Avenues, Pearson Park, Newland, Spring Bank, the Fruit Market and Hull's bay-fronted terraces.
- **Detailed checklists** of what is included.
- **Online booking / instant quotes** (Spify, Elite Oven Cleaners via Reservio).

### Where they are weak (our opportunity)

- **National sites are generic.** Hello Cleaners, New Park and End of Tenancy Cleaners use near-identical template text with the town name swapped in. They have no real local team, photos or reviews from that town.
- **Local firms have thin websites.** Most of the 20 local companies have one-paragraph service pages, no prices, no FAQs, no checklists, and several have broken or redirected sites (Detailed Cleaners 404, Green Commercial redirects to FM Plus).
- **Almost nobody combines all three:** genuinely local + transparent pricing + deep, helpful content.
- **Few local firms publish guides** (e.g. "how much does end of tenancy cleaning cost in Hull?", "what letting agents check at check-out").

**Our position:** *the local Hull company with national-quality content* — real local team, real photos, real prices, real reviews, and the most helpful pages in the area.

## 8. About Page (`/about/`)

The About page builds trust (E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). Google and customers both look for it.

### Content

1. **Our story:** why the company was founded, the founder's background, why Hull and East Yorkshire. Use a real founder photo and a personal note.
2. **Our mission & values:** reliability, honesty, local employment, fair pay, eco products.
3. **What makes us different:**
   - Directly employed, trained, DBS-checked cleaners (not gig workers)
   - Transparent, published prices
   - Written guarantees
   - Same cleaner every visit for regular clients
4. **Meet the team:** photos, names, roles, areas covered, short bios (links to `/our-team/`).
5. **Credentials & accreditations** (logos):
   - Public liability insurance (recommend £5–10m), employer's liability
   - DBS checks
   - BICSc training / membership (British Institute of Cleaning Science)
   - CHAS or SafeContractor (needed for commercial contracts)
   - ISO 9001 / 14001 (longer term, for commercial tenders)
   - Waste carrier licence (if removing waste)
   - Hull & Humber Chamber of Commerce membership
   - Living Wage Employer accreditation (strong differentiator)
6. **Community & sustainability:** local sponsorships, eco products, low-emission vans, charity partners.
7. **Numbers:** homes cleaned, reviews, years of combined experience. Update these over time.
8. **Company details:** registered company number, VAT number, registered address (builds trust and matches Google Business Profile).
9. **CTA:** Get a price / Join our team.

## 11. Local SEO (the most important part for Map Pack rankings)

### 11.1 Google Business Profile (GBP)

- Verified profile with a **real address**: a small office/unit, not a virtual office. Map Pack rankings depend heavily on proximity, so a central Hull location is ideal. A second verified location in Beverley or Bridlington can be added later.
- Primary category: **House cleaning service** (or "Cleaning service"). Add secondary categories: Commercial cleaning service, Carpet cleaning service, Window cleaning service, etc. as services are added.
- Every service listed in GBP with description and price.
- Service areas set for all towns.
- **Weekly GBP posts** (offers, before/after, tips).
- **20+ real photos at launch**, then new photos weekly (team, vans, jobs).
- Q&A section seeded with real customer questions.
- Booking link to the online booking page.

### 11.2 Reviews engine

Reviews are the biggest local ranking and conversion factor. Competitors lead with 147–178 reviews (Normans, Dirty Exterior, RH).

- **Target:** 50 Google reviews in the first 3 months, 150+ in 12 months.
- Automated SMS/email review request sent 2 hours after every job (e.g. via BrightLocal, NiceJob or Trustindex).
- Ask customers to mention the **service and town** in their review (helps relevance).
- Reply to **every** review within 24 hours.
- Also collect reviews on Trustpilot, Facebook, Yell and Checkatrade / MyBuilder.

### 11.3 Citations (directory listings)

Identical Name, Address and Phone (NAP) on: Yell, Thomson Local, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, Checkatrade, TrustATrader, MyBuilder, Bark, Nextdoor, Scoot, FreeIndex, Cylex, Hotfrog, 192.com, Hull Daily Mail directory, Hull & Humber Chamber of Commerce directory.

### 11.4 Local links & PR

- **Partnerships:** letting agents, estate agents, builders, kitchen fitters, removal companies, Airbnb managers, caravan parks. Cross-referrals and "recommended suppliers" page links.
- **Sponsorships:** local junior football/rugby clubs, Hull Fair, charity events. These earn links from club and event websites.
- **Local press:** Hull Daily Mail / Hull Live and BBC Radio Humberside stories (e.g. "Hull cleaner price survey 2026", charity deep cleans for the elderly, jobs created).
- **University of Hull:** student accommodation partner listings, move-out cleaning deals.
- **Business networks:** Hull & Humber Chamber, FSB, BNI chapters.
- **Linkable assets:** annual "Cost of a cleaner in Hull" report, free checklists, mould-in-terraces guide.

## 12. Technical SEO

- **Fast, modern build** (e.g. Next.js / Astro or a well-optimised WordPress). Target Core Web Vitals "Good" on mobile; aim for LCP < 2.0s.
- **Mobile-first design.** Most cleaning searches happen on phones.
- **Schema markup (structured data):**
  - `LocalBusiness` (homepage + area pages; schema.org has no dedicated house-cleaning type)
  - `Service` (each service page, with `areaServed` and `offers`)
  - `FAQPage` (FAQ sections; Google now shows FAQ rich results only for a few authoritative sites, so treat this as a nice-to-have)
  - No self-serving `Review` / `AggregateRating` markup about the business. Google ignores it; reviews belong on Google Business Profile
  - `BreadcrumbList`, `Organization`, `Person` (authors)
  - Full implementation details: [`technical-spec-wordpress.md`](./technical-spec-wordpress.md)
- Clean URLs, one H1 per page, unique title tags and meta descriptions:
  - Example title: `End of Tenancy Cleaning Hull | From £150 | Deposit-Back Guarantee | [Brand]`
- XML sitemap + robots.txt; submit to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- **Internal linking plan:** every guide links to its pillar service page; every service page links to related services and area pages; breadcrumbs everywhere.
- Image optimisation (WebP/AVIF, descriptive file names and alt text, e.g. `end-of-tenancy-kitchen-hull-hu5.webp`).
- HTTPS, canonical tags, no duplicate content across location pages.
- **Analytics:** GA4, Google Search Console, call tracking (e.g. CallRail), conversion events for quote, booking, call and WhatsApp.
