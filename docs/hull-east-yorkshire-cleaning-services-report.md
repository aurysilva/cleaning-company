# Cleaning Services Market Research — Hull & East Yorkshire

**Prepared:** 14 October 2026
**Purpose:** Identify the services most commonly offered by leading cleaning companies in Hull and the East Riding of Yorkshire, to decide which high-demand services a new cleaning company should launch with.

---

## 1. Summary

The 20 cleaning companies reviewed fall into three groups:

- **Interior / property cleaners** (domestic, tenancy, office). This is the largest group and the most competitive.
- **Exterior cleaning specialists** (gutters, roofs, driveways, windows). There are fewer of them, but they have some of the highest Google review counts in the area.
- **Commercial / facilities contractors** (contract, industrial, school cleaning). These are larger firms bidding for long-term contracts.

The services offered most often are **deep cleaning, office cleaning and commercial/contract cleaning** (each offered by 12 of the 20 companies), followed by **end of tenancy cleaning** (10 of 20). These are clearly where established demand is in the region.

**Recommendation in one line:** Launch with domestic regular cleaning, deep cleaning, end of tenancy and after-builders cleaning, plus small office cleaning. Then add carpet & upholstery and oven cleaning as add-on services. Consider holiday-let changeovers if you want to cover the coast (Bridlington / Hornsea).

---

## 2. Methodology

- **Area covered:** Kingston upon Hull plus the East Riding (Beverley, Cottingham, Hessle, Bridlington, Driffield, Hornsea/Skipsea, Goole/Howden).
- **How the 20 companies were selected:** high Google / Yell ratings and review volume, appearance in local "best of" rankings (Three Best Rated, TopRatedHouseCleaners.co.uk, Google Places results), and a mix of company types and locations so the sample reflects the whole market, not just one segment.
- **How services were counted:** each company's website (or directory listing where the website was unavailable) was reviewed. Similar services were grouped into one category (for example "jet washing", "pressure washing" and "driveway & patio cleaning" all count as one). A company counts towards a service only if it actively advertises it.
- **Demand indicator:** how many of the top 20 companies offer each service. Google review counts are used as a secondary signal of customer volume.

### Limitations

- "Top" is based on online reputation and visibility, not turnover. Private revenue figures are not public.
- Some websites list only headline services. Small firms may do more than they advertise.
- Detailed Cleaners Hull's website was offline (404), so their directory listing was used. Green Commercial Cleaning's website now redirects to its parent group FM Plus, so its Three Best Rated listing was used.
- Become Clean has recently rebranded as **BCS North**.

## 5. Key Findings

### 5.1 The core of the market is interior property cleaning
Deep cleaning, end of tenancy, regular domestic and office cleaning appear across almost every generalist firm. Customers expect these services, and they need little equipment, so they are the natural starting point. They are also crowded, so service quality, reliability and reviews will decide who wins.

### 5.2 Landlords and letting agents are a major customer group
At least 10 of the 20 firms target tenancy turnovers, and several (Esteem, GraceBuilt, Become Clean, JK) specifically mention HMOs, serviced apartments, student accommodation and letting agents. Hull's large private rental and student market makes **end of tenancy + after-builders cleaning** a strong recurring revenue stream through B2B relationships.

### 5.3 Exterior cleaning has very strong customer demand
Exterior specialists have some of the **highest review counts in the region**: Dirty Exterior Cleaning (178), Normans Cleaning (147) and Exterior Cleaning Co (56). This suggests high job volume. These services need more investment (pressure washers, gutter vacuums such as SkyVac, water-fed poles, working-at-height insurance), but they make good later expansions.

### 5.4 Oven cleaning is an under-served niche
Only 3 of the 20 firms offer oven cleaning, yet **OvenGleamers Beverley is the #1 ranked "house cleaner" in Hull with 113 five-star reviews**, almost entirely from oven cleaning. Demand is high, there are few competitors, and startup costs are low. This is a clear add-on opportunity.

### 5.5 Holiday lets drive demand on the coast
Spify (Bridlington), Ortus Cleans (Hornsea/Skipsea) and others build their business around **holiday-let and Airbnb changeovers**, often with linen supply. Bridlington changeover prices are published at **£85 (1-bed) to £275 (5-bed)**. Reflections Cleaning (Beverley) also gets repeat work cleaning **static caravans**, which is relevant given the caravan parks along the Holderness coast.

### 5.6 Large commercial contracts are dominated by established firms
School, industrial/warehouse, washroom and multi-site contract work is mainly held by large, accredited operators (ICS: 850+ staff, ISO 9001/14001; Green Commercial: ISO 9001/14001/45001; Minster: national franchise). A new company should target **small offices, shops, pubs, salons and communal areas** first, rather than competing directly for large tenders.

## 7. Recommendations for a New Cleaning Company

### Phase 1 — Launch services (low equipment cost, highest demand)
1. **Regular domestic cleaning** (weekly / fortnightly): steady recurring income.
2. **Deep cleaning / one-off cleans**: the most widely offered service and an easy way for customers to try you.
3. **End of tenancy cleaning**: build relationships with Hull letting agents, landlords and HMO operators.
4. **After-builders cleaning**: partner with local builders, developers and renovation firms.
5. **Small office & commercial cleaning**: offices, shops, salons, pubs, communal areas; recurring contracts.

### Phase 2 — Add-on services (raise the value of each job)
6. **Oven cleaning**: under-served, high demand, low equipment cost.
7. **Carpet & upholstery cleaning**: one machine (e.g. Prochem-type extractor) opens up both domestic and landlord upsells.
8. **Holiday let / Airbnb changeovers**: especially if covering Bridlington, Hornsea, Withernsea and caravan parks.

### Phase 3 — Expansion (needs more equipment, training and insurance)
9. **Exterior cleaning bundle**: gutters / fascias / soffits, driveways & patios, then windows, roof and render soft washing.
10. **Specialist work**: emergency / sanitisation cleans, solar panels, communal contracts for housing associations.

### What the best-rated firms have in common
- **Insurance and trust:** public liability insurance (Crystal Clear advertises £5m+), DBS-checked staff and key-holding.
- **Flexible hours:** 7 days a week, including early mornings and evenings (RH 7am–8pm; iClean 8am–8pm).
- **Free, no-obligation quotes**, with clear upfront pricing (Spify uses flat-rate pricing by property layout).
- **Eco-friendly products** as a selling point (RH, Esteem, Crystal Clear, GraceBuilt).
- **Directly employed staff** rather than gig workers (Spify, ICS).
- **Visible Google reviews:** the firms with the most reviews dominate local rankings, so build a review-collection habit from day one.
